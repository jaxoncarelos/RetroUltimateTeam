1. **Defensive adaptation (owner's top request).** The CPU defense must learn, so repeating the same play or formation stops working and forces you to switch. Add `scripts/match/ai/defense_memory.gd` (pure, unit-tested). Track your recent calls by play id, formation and category, and their yards gained, with decay over time. The defensive play caller should weight counter-calls: vs RUN → run blitz / goal line / 8 in the box; vs SHORT → press man / cover 2; vs DEEP → cover 3 / cover 1 deep. When the same play repeats (2+ times in the last ~5 calls, or after a big gain), the defense is "keyed": keyed defenders get reduced reaction time and jump the repeated routes (bias zone/man aims toward that play's route points, raise INT/deflection chances), and blitzes hit faster vs a repeated run. The HUD warns "DEFENSE IS KEYING ON <PLAY>". Tune with `--sim` so spamming one play averages clearly less than mixing. Put all constants in GameplayTuning or a new defense-AI tuning .tres.
